Momentum is a privacy-friendly, offline-first way to track your progress and turn everyday photos into a beautiful progress montage — without thinking about storage, editing, or accounts.

📸 Snap a photo a day
Create a project for anything you want to track — fitness, skincare, a beard, a plant, a painting, a trip. Momentum nudges you with a daily reminder so you never miss a day. Add photos from the gallery or take one right in the app with the built-in camera.

🎬 Make montages in seconds
Pick your photos and Momentum renders a smooth montage video for you. No timeline scrubbing, no export menus, no video editor to learn. Adjust frames per image, frames per second, video quality, background color, date style, and text font, then export and share your progress video.

😊 Auto face stabilization
Powered by on-device face detection, Momentum keeps faces aligned and stable across your montage, so the result looks polished even when you were not perfectly framed. Optionally highlight or censor faces with a single tap.

📅 Calendar and timeline
Browse everything you have captured in a beautiful monthly calendar or a linear timeline, mark favorites, and add a comment to any day to remember the context.

🔔 Reminders that actually work
Set a daily reminder time and Momentum will keep you on track. Reminders survive reboots, and tapping a notification jumps straight back into the app.

🎨 Yours to make it yours
Material You dynamic color from your wallpaper, AMOLED dark mode, multiple palette styles, custom fonts, and a full Material 3 Expressive interface that adapts from phones to tablets and foldables.

🔒 Private by design
Everything is processed and stored on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads. Momentum is open source and available as a FOSS build with all features unlocked and no in-app purchases.

💾 Backup and restore
Export all of your project data to a single .zip file and import it back whenever you like. Move to a new phone without losing your history.

Momentum Plus unlocks extra customization (video quality, custom fonts, background colors, AMOLED, custom palettes) and lets you keep as many projects as you want. Every purchase supports independent development.
